Giant Cinnamon Roll

Yield: 1 giant 9-inch roll • Soft, gooey, shareable

Quantity

Scale:
All-purpose flour: 300 g
Sugar: 50 g
Melted butter: 60 g
Warm milk: 180 ml
Egg: 1 pc

Method

  1. Warm the milk (not hot). Mix in sugar and yeast, rest 5 min.
  2. Whisk in melted butter and egg.
  3. Add flour and salt. Knead 8–10 min until smooth.
  4. Let rise ~1 hour. Roll dough, spread butter + cinnamon sugar, roll up into a spiral.
  5. Let it rise again in the pan, then bake.

Notes / Tips:

  • Dough will feel a bit sticky. Don’t panic and don’t dump extra flour.
  • Milk should be warm, not hot, or the yeast dies.
  • Tighter roll = taller cinnamon roll, looser roll = flatter, more pull-apart.
Bake at 180°C for 22–25 minutes (fan oven, middle rack)
