Ingredients




Quantity

Scale:
All-purpose flour: 300 g
Sugar: 50 g
Butter (melted): 60 g
Milk (warm): 180 ml

Method

  1. Warm the milk (not hot). Mix in sugar and yeast, rest 5 min.
  2. Whisk in melted butter and egg.
  3. Add flour and salt. Knead 8–10 min until smooth.
  4. Let rise ~1 hour, roll out, fill, shape, rise again, bake.
Bake at 180°C for 22–25 minutes (fan oven, middle rack)
